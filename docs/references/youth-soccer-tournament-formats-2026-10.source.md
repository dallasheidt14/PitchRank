# Youth Soccer Tournament Formats by Number of Teams: A Field Guide (US focus, rules current as of October 2026)

The core finding: for almost every team count, US youth soccer builds the schedule out of **groups of 4 (and, when needed, groups of 3) so that every team gets its 3 guaranteed games**, then picks the knockout size (final only, semifinals, or quarterfinals) based on how many "spots" are open, filling any gaps with **wildcards**. The best single reference is the US Youth Soccer (USYS) Presidents Cup protocol, which publishes an official template for every count from 3 to 16.\[1\] Big events (Surf Cup, Arizona State Cup, ECNL, MLS NEXT, GA) all use variations of that same logic. Your "12 teams" example is almost always **3 groups of 4 → 3 group winners + 1 wildcard → semifinals → final**, though 4 groups of 3 and 2 groups of 6 also turn up.

## TL;DR
- **The default building block is the 4-team round robin, where every team plays everyone else in its group once (3 games each).** Odd counts get fixed with 3-team groups. Those groups either play a "crossover" (they play the other 3-team group instead of their own) or play only 2 games, with points averaged. USYS publishes ready-made templates for every count from 3 to 16 teams, and most tournaments copy them.
- **How many teams advance depends on how many groups there are.** 2 groups → top 2 from each to semifinals (or winners straight to a final). 3 groups → winners + 1 wildcard to semifinals. 4 groups → winners to semifinals. 5–6 groups → winners + wildcards to quarterfinals. 8+ groups → round of 16. Very large events (ECNL, MLS NEXT Cup, GA) use "best second-place / best third-place" spots or straight knockout brackets.
- **The fine print decides who advances, and it varies by event.** Points systems are 3-1-0 (national events) or the "10-point" system (6 for a win, 3 for a tie, +1 per goal up to 3, +1 for a shutout). Goal-difference caps range from +3 to +5 or none. Head-to-head is dropped for wildcard races. Knockout ties now usually go straight to penalties: the USYS 2025-26 competition rules say knockout matches "will proceed directly to Penalty Kicks if tied, with no Extra Time played." Always read the specific event's rules PDF for the current season.

## Key Terms (plain English)
- **Cohort / flight / division / bracket:** the set of teams in one age group and level that compete for one trophy (e.g., "U13 Boys Gold"). In GotSport, a "division" can contain multiple "brackets" (= groups).\[2\] Many US rulebooks also say "bracket" when they mean "group," and Surf Cup's rules say "Bracket A" for Group A.\[3\]
- **Pool play / group play / bracket play:** the first stage, where teams play the others in their small group. Results count toward standings.
- **Round robin:** everyone in a group plays everyone else once. A group of *n* teams needs n×(n−1)/2 games.
- **Crossover:** teams in Group A play only teams in Group B (and vice versa), but standings are still kept inside each team's own group, or sometimes in one combined table. It's used so 3-team groups can still give everyone 3 games.
- **Wildcard:** an extra playoff spot for the best non-winner across all groups, chosen by points and then tiebreakers.
- **Seeding:** ranking teams before the draw (or before knockouts) so the strongest don't meet early.
- **Reseeding / "shuffling":** rearranging knockout matchups after group play, usually to avoid an immediate rematch of a group game.
- **Consolation / placement bracket (Gold/Silver, A/B playoffs):** a second knockout for teams that didn't make the main one.
- **Showcase format:** usually one game a day for college-scouting exposure. Some events crown no champion, while others still rank teams on points.
- **Kicks from the penalty mark (KFTPM):** the penalty shootout.

## Key Findings

1. **USYS publishes the most complete official "format per team count" catalog.** The 2024-25 Presidents Cup Competition Protocols (Protocol 515) say brackets should be even where possible, "A minimum of three (3) teams is required to make a bracket," and give day-by-day templates for 3 through 16 teams.\[1\] They're reproduced in the catalog below.
2. **Unequal group sizes are handled three ways:**
   - **Crossover:** the 3-team groups play each other (USYS 6-, 10- and 14-team templates; Nebraska; Surf Cup 6-team flights).\[1\]\[4\]\[5\]
   - **Byes plus points normalization:** USYS 7-, 11- and 13-team templates. "For teams that play two (2) group matches, the average number of points earned from those two (2) matches will be added to their point total for purposes of determining advancement."\[1\]
   - **Per-game averages, or counting only the first 3 games** (an Eau Claire, WI tournament).\[6\]\[7\]
3. **Wildcard races drop head-to-head**, since the teams never played each other, and they block an immediate rematch. The USYS East Region says the wildcard is chosen using the same criteria "without head to head competition" and that "The wildcard team cannot play a team from their group in the initial knock-out round." Surf Cup will "shuffle wildcard seeds" to avoid a group-stage rematch.\[3\]\[8\]
4. **Rules changed for 2025-26/2026:**
   - Per a US Youth Soccer press release (Frisco, TX, Sept. 23, 2025), State Cup champions from all 54 USYS State Associations now advance directly to the National Championships, "eliminating the traditional Regional Championship round," alongside group winners from The National League Winter Events. Nationals is now split into Super and Premier Groups.
   - The USYS 2025-26 National Championships and Presidents Cup Competition Rules say "Knockout Round matches, Semi-Finals, and the Final will proceed directly to Penalty Kicks if tied, with no Extra Time played." Flicktec's explainer quotes the same rule and notes that general USYS playing rules still use 2×10-minute overtime at 11U–14U and 2×15 at 15U–19U.
   - In 2023-24, the East Region still played two 10–15-minute overtime periods.\[8\]
5. **Scheduling software constrains format but doesn't dictate it.** GotSport only offers templates matching the bracket's team count. Anything non-standard, such as unequal game counts, requires building a custom template.\[9\]\[10\] I couldn't find public template lists for TGS or SincSports.

## The Catalog, Organized by Number of Teams

*Unless noted, "games per team" means group-stage games, plus 1–2 more for teams that advance. "Total games" counts every match in the flight. All counts assume one meeting per pair.*

### 3 teams
- **A. Round robin + final (USYS official):** each team plays 2 games, on days 1–3 with one bye day each. The top 2 meet in a final.\[1\] Total 4 games. If you finish 3rd you only get 2 games, which breaks the usual 3-game guarantee.
- **B. Double round robin, no final:** everyone plays everyone twice (4 games each, 6 total), and the top team on points wins. Used by Nebraska's Centris Cup ("Each team will play round robin games twice (each team gets four games)")\[11\] and by Gothia-style floorball events. A 3v3 operator (The Soccer Showcase) has used this before.
- **C. Round robin + "points match" (3v3 variant):** after 2 games, #1 goes straight to the final. #2 and #3 play a third game for points (not elimination), and whoever then leads advances. Total 5 games. Used by The Soccer Showcase 3v3 for U8 in 2024.\[12\]
- **D. Merge with another flight.** Many directors avoid 3-team flights altogether by combining ages or levels, or by adding a "guest team." Surf Cup's guest teams "cannot advance out of Bracket" and their results are recorded as a 1-0 loss.\[13\]

### 4 teams
- **A. Round robin, no final:** 3 games each, 6 total. Group winner is champion. Used at NL Cup Finals 4-team groups ("The NL Cup Champion will be the first-place finisher in the four-team group")\[14\] and at Surf College Cup 4-team flights.\[15\]
- **B. Round robin + final (1 vs 2):** 4 games for finalists, 7 total. This is the USYS template. The East Region says "If only four teams entered then there will be a round robin followed by a final between the top two teams," and Western WA Surf Invitational uses it too.\[1\]\[8\]\[16\]
- **C. Round robin + final + 3rd-place game (3 vs 4):** every team plays 4 games, 8 total. Common when fields allow.
- **D. 4-team knockout (semifinals + final + consolation):** 2 games each, so it fails the 3-game guarantee unless paired with group games. Mostly seen as the playoff stage after pools.

### 5 teams
- **A. Full round robin, no final (USYS official):** 4 games each, 10 total, run over 5 days with one bye each. "The Champion and Finalist are determined by standings at the conclusion of the round robin." Surf Cup uses this for its 5-team flights (4 games each).\[1\]\[5\]
- **B. Full round robin + final (top 2):** 5 games for finalists, 11 total. Western WA Surf Invitational: "teams play all other teams in the bracket in a four-game round-robin schedule, and the 2 top point leaders will advance to the finals."\[16\]
- **C. Partial round robin, 3 games each:** 5 teams × 3 games is an odd number of slots, so one team must play 4. Eau Claire's rule: "One team in each 5 Team Bracket will play 4 games. This team's points will be determined by its first three games."\[6\] Total about 8 games, plus a final if one is used.
- **D. 2 games each, then play-in + semifinals (3v3/4v4 variant):** all 5 teams are seeded after 2 games. #4 plays #5 in a play-in, #2 plays #3, and #1 plays the play-in winner.\[17\] Total about 9 games (The Soccer Showcase 4v4).\[12\]
- **E. Two groups of 5 (leagues/cups):** ECNL North Atlantic League Cup runs "Two groups of five, with the top two from each group advancing" (a 10-team example; see 10 teams).\[18\]\[19\]

### 6 teams
- **A. Two groups of 3, full crossover + semifinals (USYS official):** each team plays all 3 teams in the *other* group. "The four (4) highest point totals (regardless of group) advance to the semifinal round. Semifinal pairings are Highest Points vs. 4th Highest Points and 2nd Highest Points vs. 3rd Highest Points. Semi-finalists could have played each other during group play."\[1\] 9 group games + 3 = 12 total; 3–5 games per team.
- **B. Two groups of 3: 2 games in your own group + 1 crossover, then the top 4 overall go to semifinals.** The East Region (2023-24) splits into two sub-groups of three "for scheduling purposes only." Tiebreaks skip head-to-head "unless all teams played each other."\[8\]
- **C. Crossover, then top 2 straight to a final:** 10 total. Surf Cup 2025 Youngers: teams play the opposite bracket for 3 games, "with the top two... (wildcards) from any bracket advancing to the Championship Game." Western WA Surf Invitational uses the same structure.\[5\]\[16\]
- **D. Two groups of 3, own-group round robin only (2 games), then semifinals A1–B2 / B1–A2, final, and a 3A vs 3B consolation:** 10 total (The Soccer Showcase 3v3).\[12\]\[17\]
- **E. Single group of 6, full round robin:** 5 games each, 15 total. Only feasible over 4+ days or with short games, and rare at weekend events.
- **F. Single group of 6, partial round robin (3 games each):** 9 games, then a final. Eau Claire's 6-team bracket plays "three pool games, including one cross pool game."\[6\]

### 7 teams
- **A. Group of 4 + group of 3, winners + 2 wildcards to semifinals (USYS official):** the 3-team group plays only 2 games each and gets the "average points" bump. Semifinals are A winner vs higher wildcard and B winner vs lower wildcard, swapped if needed.\[1\] 9 group games + 3 = 12 total.
- **B. Group of 4 + group of 3, with "ghost" crossover games** so the 3-team group gets a third game against Group A teams. Some Group A teams then play 4 games, and organizers count only 3 or use per-game averages. This is common at local weekend events and isn't standardized.
- **C. 2 games each, seed 1–7, #1 gets a bye, then quarterfinals 2v7, 3v6, 4v5:** "if the pairings create a re-match… teams MAY be re-seeded" (The Soccer Showcase 3v3).\[12\]\[17\] 7 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 13 total.

### 8 teams
- **A. Two groups of 4, group winners straight to a final (A1 vs B1):** 4 games for finalists, 13 total. Used by Surf Cup 8-team flights ("top team in each bracket advancing to the Final") and Western WA Surf Invitational.\[16\]\[20\]
- **B. Two groups of 4 + crossover semifinals (A1–B2, B1–A2) + final (USYS official; East Region):**\[1\]\[8\] 12 + 3 = 15 total. Gothia and Dana Cup groups also send the top 2 forward.\[21\]\[22\]
- **C. Two groups of 4, all advance (Gold and Silver brackets):** Gold semifinals A1–B2 and B1–A2; Silver semifinals A3–B4 and A4–B3. Everyone gets 5 games, 18 total (The Soccer Showcase "8 TEAM BRACKET SPECIAL FORMAT").\[12\] Gothia's A/B playoffs and Dana Cup's A/B finals do the same thing at scale.\[23\]\[24\]
- **D. Full 4×4 crossover:** Group A teams play only Group B teams (3 games each, 12 total), and standings are kept by group or overall. This is the 6-team crossover logic stretched to 8. It's used where organizers want two "strength tiers" to meet. I didn't find a published major-event template for it.
- **E. 8-team straight knockout (quarterfinals):** 7 games, or 12 with a full consolation bracket. Used at the NL Cup Finals: "Teams play three games in three days in a bracket format, where games progress from quarterfinals to semifinals to the finals."\[25\] Rare at club events, because losers get 1 game unless there's consolation.

### 9 teams
- **A. Three groups of 3, round robin (2 games each), winners + 1 wildcard to semifinals (USYS official):** 9 + 3 = 12 total. Semifinals are A–B and C–wildcard, and "If the Wildcard comes from Group C" the pairing flips.\[1\]
- **B. Three groups of 3 + a third crossover game for #2 and #3 teams** (#2A–#3C, #2B–#3A, #2C–#3B). Group winners and one wildcard go to semifinals. 9 + 3 + 3 = 15 total (The Soccer Showcase 3v3).\[12\]
- **C. Group of 4 + group of 5, or three groups of 3 with full crossover**, at local events to hit 3 games. Not standardized.

### 10 teams
- **A. One group of 4 + two groups of 3 that cross over (USYS official):** "The 4-team group winner and the next three (3) highest point totals (regardless of group) advance to the semifinal round."\[1\] 6 + 9 = 15 group games + 3 = 18 total. Semifinal rematches are allowed.
- **B. One group of 4 + two groups of 3 with crossover; the 3 group winners + 1 wildcard go to semifinals.** "There cannot be a re-match in the semifinal" (Nebraska Level 2 and Centris Cup rules).\[4\]\[11\] The Soccer Showcase 3v3 uses the same structure.\[12\]
- **C. Two groups of 5 (top 2 from each to semifinals):** 20 group games (4 each) + 3 = 23 total. Used by ECNL North Atlantic League Cup (2025-26)\[18\]\[19\] and by multi-weekend league cups. Too many games for a weekend.
- **D. Two groups of 5, partial play (3 games each)** with a 1-team-plays-4 workaround as for 5 teams. Typical at weekend events, and not standardized.

### 11 teams
- **A. Two groups of 4 + one group of 3; winners + 1 wildcard to semifinals (USYS official):** the 3-team group gets 2 games plus the average-points bump.\[1\] 15 + 3 = 18 total.
- **B. The same split with the 3-team group "crossing" into a 4-team group for a third game.** Local variant.

### 12 teams (the user's example)
- **A. Three groups of 4; 3 winners + 1 wildcard to semifinals: the dominant US format.** 18 group games + 3 = 21 total; 3–5 games per team.
  - **USYS Presidents Cup official template.** "If the Wildcard comes from Group C then the semifinal parings are Group A Winner vs Group C Winner and Group B Winner vs. Wildcard."\[1\]
  - **USYS East Region:** semifinals are A winner vs wildcard and B winner vs C winner.\[8\]
  - **Surf Cup (2025 and 2026):** "Winner of Bracket A against the wildcard, and the winner of Bracket B against the winner of Bracket C".\[3\]\[20\]
  - **FC Dallas Bobby Rhine Invitational:** "Divisions with 12 teams will have the highest point earners in each group advance and a wild card team."\[26\]
- **B. Three groups of 4; winners + top runners-up to quarterfinals.** 18 + 7 = 25 total. Nomads Soccer's 12-team rule uses group winners and wildcards in quarterfinals, e.g., "The winner of Group C will [play] Wildcard #2 in Quarterfinal #2."\[27\]
- **C. Four groups of 3; group winners to semifinals.** Only 2 group games each, so teams need crossovers to reach 3. Seen in Virginia (VYSA) Presidents Cup materials, which refer to "Play Groups A, C & D in a 12-team age group."\[28\] Less common because of the 3-game guarantee.
- **D. Two groups of 6 with partial play (3 games each), then top 2 → semifinals or winners → final.** Used occasionally by local events. Copa Sul-Sudeste (adult, Brazil) uses 2 groups of 6 at league scale.\[29\]
- **E. 12-team "league phase"** (each team plays 3–4 pre-assigned opponents in one table). See Swiss/league phase below.

### 13 teams
- **One group of 4 + three groups of 3; group winners to semifinals (USYS official).** The 3-team groups get byes (2 games each).\[1\] 6 + 9 = 15 + 3 = 18 total.

### 14 teams
- **A. Two groups of 4 + two groups of 3 that cross over; 4-team group winners + the 2 best crossover teams to semifinals (USYS official).**\[1\] 6 + 6 + 9 = 21 + 3 = 24 total.
- **B. The same split with the 4 group winners to semifinals.** Nomads' 14-team rule: "two (2) groups of four (4) which will play within their bracket… and two (2) groups of three (3) which will play crossover."\[27\] A baseball/softball pool-play template describes the same structure.\[30\]

### 15 teams
- **Three groups of 4 + one group of 3; group winners to semifinals (USYS official).**\[1\] 18 + 3 = 21 + 3 = 24 total.

### 16 teams
- **A. Four groups of 4, winners to semifinals.** 24 + 3 = 27 total; 3–5 games each. Used by the USYS official template (A–B, C–D), the East Region (A–D, B–C), Surf Cup,\[1\]\[3\]\[8\] and ECNL Texas League Cup ("Four groups of four, with each group winner advancing to the knockout stage").\[18\]\[19\]
- **B. Four groups of 4, top 2 to quarterfinals.** 24 + 7 = 31 total; up to 6 games, so needs 4+ days. World Cup-style, and used at longer events (Gothia/Dana A-playoffs from groups).
- **C. Gothia "league phase" of 16:** G17 at Gothia 2026 is "a single league consisting of 16 teams. All teams play four matches." Places 1–4 go to A playoffs, 5–8 to B playoffs, and the rest play a placement match.\[31\]

### 18 teams
- **Three groups of 4 + two groups of 3 (combined/crossover), then quarterfinals (USYS East Region).** "Game #1 Runner up Of Group A v. Winner of Groups D/E (combined)…"\[8\] About 27 + 7 = 34 total.

### 20 teams
- **A. Five groups of 4; 5 winners + 3 wildcards to quarterfinals (USYS East Region, Surf Thanksgiving).** 30 + 7 = 37 total. "A group winner cannot play a wildcard team from their original bracket in the Quarter-Final Round."\[8\]\[32\]
- **B. 20-team field into 5 groups of 4, top 16 to the next stage (GA Champions Cup 2025-26).** The "Top 3 teams from each group… advance to Final 16" plus the "Best 4th place team"; the other 4 drop to a Showcase group.\[33\]\[34\]

### 24 teams
- **A. Six groups of 4; 6 winners + 2 wildcards to quarterfinals.** 36 + 7 = 43 total. Used by the USYS East Region and Surf Thanksgiving ("top team in each Bracket along with two (2) Wildcard Teams advancing to the Quarter Finals").\[8\]\[32\]
- **B. Six groups of 4; top 2 + 4 best third-place teams to the round of 16 (GA Champions Cup 2026-27, 24 per age group).** "Top two teams from each group advance – The four best third-place teams advance – 16 teams advance to the Champions Cup Finals – Teams 17–20 advance to the Champions Cup Showcase."\[35\] 36 group games + 15 = 51 knockout-inclusive. GA's Finals are a "four-game championship format."\[35\]
- **C. 24-team league phase (Gothia B17 2026):** 4 matches each; 1–4 go to A playoffs, 5–8 to B playoffs, the rest to placement games.\[36\]

### 32 teams
- **A. 32-team single-elimination with a consolation bracket (ECNL Boys U18/19 Playoffs, June 2026):** "Winners from the round of 32 will advance to the round of 16… while the 16 teams who lose in their opening game will move to the North American Cup bracket."\[37\] 31 main games + consolation.
- **B. Straight 32-team knockout (MLS NEXT Cup 2026, U15–U19 Homegrown).** "A single-elimination competition, 32 teams per age group."\[38\]\[39\]
- **C. Eight groups of 4; top 2 to round of 16, or winners to quarterfinals.** 48 + 15 = 63, or 48 + 7 = 55. Common at large-event age groups; mirrors the old World Cup.

### 48 teams
- **A. Twelve groups of 4; 12 winners + 4 best runners-up to round of 16 (ECNL Boys U13 Playoffs, 2026).** 72 + 15 = 87 total.\[37\]
- **B. Twelve groups of 4; top 2 + 8 best third-place teams to round of 32 (FIFA World Cup 2026 model).** Not seen in youth play.
- **C. MLS NEXT Cup 2027** (June 5–13, 2027): MLSsoccer.com's 2026-27 schedule says "The Allstate Homegrown Division will expand from 32 to 48 teams in each of the U15 through U19 age groups, with teams qualifying based on league play, the Pro Player Pathway competition or MLS NEXT Flex." The exact 48-team bracket hadn't been published as of this writing.

### 64+ teams
- **A. ECNL Boys U14–U17 Playoffs (Surf Sports Park, San Diego, June 25–July 2, 2026):** ECNL's June 22, 2026 preview says "The U14-U17 age groups each have 66 teams divided into 15 groups of four and two groups of three." The two 3-team-group winners play each other for the final round-of-16 spot, then two knockout rounds decide the final four for the ECNL Finals in Richmond.
- **B. MLS NEXT Flex (qualifier):** "U15 and U17 (16 groups) and U16 and U19 (13 groups)" of four. Per SoccerWire, that came to 232 teams at the Maryland SoccerPlex, May 9–13, 2025: 64 teams each at U15/U17 and 52 each at U16/U19. Only group winners qualify for MLS NEXT Cup. No knockout at Flex itself.
- **C. Gothia Cup / Dana Cup:** dozens of groups of 4–5. Top 2 go to an A knockout and the rest to a B knockout. Gothia says "The minimum amount of games you will play is four. The average number of matches played per team is 5,8."\[22\]\[40\]

## Details

### Guaranteed games and why "3" drives everything
- **Three games is the standard promise:**
  - Surf Cup: "All age groups and divisions are guaranteed three (3) games."\[5\]
  - Las Vegas Mayor's Cup International Tournament (Oct 2026): "Each team is guaranteed three matches."\[41\]
  - Showcase-level events promise more. Las Vegas Mayor's Cup Girls Showcase: "4 game guarantee for U15-U19, 3 game guarantee for U13-U14."\[42\]
  - Gothia promises at least 4.\[40\]
- **Why groups of 4 dominate:** a 4-team round robin delivers exactly 3 games, the smallest number that works, using 6 matches.\[43\]
- **3-team groups only give 2 games**, which forces one of three fixes:
  - a crossover with another 3-team group (USYS 6/10/14 templates);\[1\]
  - accepting 2 games and averaging points (USYS 7/11/13);\[1\]
  - sending the losers into a consolation game.
- **5-team groups give 4 games**, which is too many for a 2-day weekend. That's why tournaments use the "one team plays 4, only the first 3 count" workaround.\[6\]

### How wildcards are picked, and how unequal groups are normalized
1. **Raw points across groups:** the most common method when every team plays 3 (Surf Cup, USYS 12-team).\[1\]\[3\]
2. **Average-points bump for 2-game teams:** the USYS Presidents Cup adds the two-game average to the total, effectively converting 2 games into 3.\[1\]
3. **Per-game average:** an older Region 1 tournament ranks forfeiting/short schedules "based on the 'per game average for games actually played'".\[7\]
4. **Count only the first 3 games:** Eau Claire, for the 5-team bracket.\[6\]
5. **Simple rank-order:** one SportsEngine-hosted event picks the wildcard by "Non-group winner with the most total points," then "most wins."\[44\]
6. **Best Nth-place tables:** ECNL Boys U13 (4 best runners-up) and GA (4 best thirds).\[35\]\[37\] These are fair only if all groups are the same size, and ECNL keeps its 3-team groups separate by having those two winners play each other.\[37\]

**Head-to-head is excluded from wildcard ties** (USYS East).\[8\] After assigning wildcards, most events **re-pair the bracket so a wildcard doesn't face its own group winner** (USYS PC; USYS East; Surf Cup "shuffle"; Nebraska "There cannot be a re-match in the semifinal").\[1\]\[3\]\[4\]\[8\]

### Points systems
- **3-1-0** is the standard. It's used by USYS National Championships and Presidents Cup 2025-26, Arizona State Cup 2026, Surf Cup 2026,\[13\]\[45\] Gothia, Dana Cup, and Las Vegas Mayor's Cup.\[13\]\[22\]\[46\]\[47\]\[48\]
- **2-1-0:** still used by some local events, e.g., Nomads Soccer ("Two (2) points for each win One (1) point for each tie").\[27\]
- **"10-point system":** 6 for a win, 3 for a tie, 0 for a loss, +1 per goal (max 3), +1 for a shutout, so a maximum of 10 per game.
  - Texas United's Houston Easter Invitational (2021) uses it, with a forfeit scored "1-0 and counts as 8 qualifying points."\[49\]
  - Eau Claire uses it with "Forfeit: -10 points."\[6\]
  - Variants drop the shutout point, e.g., the UCLA "Bruin Classic" (6/3/0 + goals, max 3).\[50\]
  - **In plain English:** it rewards scoring and clean sheets, so a team can earn points in a loss (e.g., losing 3-4 earns 3). A 0-0 tie is worth 4 (3 + 1 shutout). Parents on SoCal forums regularly get confused by this.\[51\]
- **Bonus-point / capped-GD hybrids:** some older rules use "goals scored minus goals allowed - max." bonus points.\[7\]

### Tiebreaker ladders (who wins a tie on points)

| Event (season) | Order |
|---|---|
| USYS Nationals & Presidents Cup (2025-26), 2 teams | Head-to-head → most wins → GD (max +5/game) → fewest GA → fair play (fewest dismissals) → penalties (or coin toss)\[48\] |
| USYS (2025-26), 3+ teams | Head-to-head points among tied teams → most wins → head-to-head GD → overall GD → head-to-head GA → overall GA → fair play → drawing of lots\[48\] |
| USYS East Region (2023-24) | Head-to-head → most wins → GD (max 5) → fewest GA → penalties (3-way tie uses a bye-draw shootout) |
| Arizona State Cup (2026) | Head-to-head → most wins → GD (max 5) → fewest GA → fair play → penalties; 3+ tied restart at "most wins" |
| Surf Cup (2026) | Head-to-head → overall GD (no cap stated) → GF → GA → total wins → shutouts → coin toss; 3+ tied skip head-to-head\[13\] |
| Jefferson Cup Showcase | Head-to-head → GD max ±3 per game → GF (max 3/game) → GA (max 3/game) → shoot-outs\[52\] |
| Eau Claire (2026) | Total points → head-to-head → GD (max 3) → total GA |
| Las Vegas Mayor's Cup–style local (SportsEngine-hosted) | Head-to-head (2 teams only) → GD (max 4/game) → least GA → most GF (max 4) → penalties |
| Gothia / Dana Cup (Europe) | Dana: GD in all games → goals scored → head-to-head points → lots. Gothia league phase adds "combined number of points obtained by the opponents"\[22\]\[31\] |

**What this means:** US events usually put **head-to-head first**, while European events and FIFA usually put **goal difference first**. Caps on goal difference range from 3 to 5 per game (sportsmanship, so there's no reason to run up the score). Surf Cup is the notable exception that applies no stated cap.\[13\] "Most wins" ahead of goal difference (USYS, Arizona) makes the 3-1-0 system penalize ties even more.

### Seeding and flighting (how teams get sorted into levels and groups)
- **League-finish seeding:**
  - Arizona State Cup 2026: "teams which finished 1st in APL will be seeded #1, team which finished 2nd will be seeded #2, and so forth." Unseeded teams go into a "blind draw."\[45\]
  - Arizona Presidents Cup: seeds by APL, then State League 1, then State League 2 finish. "The ASA Cup Committee will determine bracket format based on participating teams."\[53\]
- **Draws:**
  - USYS East used a "double-blind draw" (states from one bowl, bracket positions from another), with prior-year semifinalist states seeded.\[8\]
  - USYS Nationals now sorts states into Super vs Premier Groups using "3-year rolling data."\[48\]
- **League-performance seeding:**
  - MLS NEXT Flex: "Teams in each age group are split into groups of four and are seeded based on their performance in league matches."\[39\]\[54\]
  - MLS NEXT U13/U14 qualify using TAKA "Quality of Play" analytics.\[54\]
- **Rankings:**
  - GotSport: "League & Tournament organizers use the rankings to ensure fair and fun competition." GotSport also rates events (Premier Elite, Premier, Platinum, Gold, Silver, Copper).\[55\]\[56\]
- **Level labels** vary by event. Weston Cup (FL) offers "MLS Next, Showcase, Gold, Silver, and Bronze," with Bronze "primarily for recreational teams."\[57\]
- *Caveat:* I couldn't verify how Youth Soccer Rankings/USA Rank or SoccerWire are used for flighting. Treat any claim that a specific tournament "uses ranking X" as something to confirm with that tournament.

### Showcase formats
- **Jefferson Cup (Richmond, VA):**
  - U9–U14 play "Championship Format on Saturday and Sunday."\[58\]
  - U16–U19 play "Showcase Format" (one game per day, Friday through Sunday).\[58\]
  - U15 choose Showcase or "Tournament Format: (two games Saturday and at least one on Sunday, Final on Sunday)".\[58\]
  - Showcase brackets still produce standings: SoccerWire's 2026 recap refers to teams finishing "top three of the U17 Championship."\[59\]
  - **In plain English:** showcase means no playoffs, but there's often still a points table and a "winner."
- **Las Vegas Mayor's Cup Boys:** "at least 4 games," described as "three bracket games and a knockout match based on your team's performance" (per a travel-agent page).\[60\]\[61\] This is a hybrid: everyone plays a 4th game, matched by group finish.
- **Showcase seating:** at the LV Mayor's Cup, "coaches and players from both teams will sit on the same sideline"\[42\] so college coaches can watch from the other side.

### Tiered placement (Gold/Silver, A/B playoffs)
- Gothia: "The two best teams in each group will advance to playoff A. The other teams in the group will advance to playoff B."\[21\]
- Dana Cup: same, with "A-Final round" and "B-Final round."\[22\]
- **US equivalents:**
  - The Soccer Showcase 8-team Gold/Silver.\[12\]
  - ECNL Boys U18/19, where round-of-32 losers move to the "North American Cup bracket."\[37\]
  - GA, where teams 17–20 go to the "Champions Cup Showcase."\[35\]
  - Dallas Cup, which offers "friendly games" to teams that "do not advance to the knock out stage," giving them "a total of at least 5 games during the week."\[62\]

### Swiss, league phase, ladder, double elimination, and "flex"
- **League phase (closest to Swiss):** Gothia 2026's G17/B17 use a single table where each team plays 4 pre-scheduled opponents (not everyone). The tiebreaker list includes opponents' combined points,\[31\]\[36\] which is a classic Swiss/Buchholz idea. I found **no US youth soccer event using true Swiss pairing**, where each round is drawn from current results.
- **Double elimination:** common in softball/baseball software, but **I found no documented youth soccer use**. It's impractical because soccer needs recovery time between games.
- **"Flex"/power-matched:** MLS NEXT "Flex" is **not** rating-matched pairing. It's a qualifier where teams are seeded into groups of four by league performance and play opponents from outside their own division. Through 2026-27, 14 "Flex Conference winners" also compete for first-round byes at MLS NEXT Cup.\[38\]\[54\] True rating-based matchmaking shows up mainly in league scheduling, not in published tournament rules I could find.
- **Ladder:** not found in youth soccer tournament rules.

### 4v4 / 7v7 / 9v9 / 11v11 differences
- **Formats by age:**
  - Las Vegas Mayor's Cup: "U10 · 7v7," "U11/U12 · 9 v 9," with U8 playing "U9/U10 rules (7v7)."\[47\]\[64\]
  - Jefferson Cup: "U10s will play 7v7 U11s & U12s will play 9v9 U13s and up will play 11v11."\[65\]
  - Texas United: U6–U7 play 4v4.\[49\]
  - Dana Cup runs separate 7:7 classes alongside 11:11.\[22\]\[66\]
- **Younger ages often keep no standings:**
  - Eau Claire: "9U & 10U: Scores will not be posted and no playoffs will be scheduled."\[6\]
  - One tournament site says "Per US Youth Soccer rules all teams U10 and younger must play only in a 'Festival-Style Format'."\[63\] This is the tournament's own claim. Many US events do crown U9–U10 champions (e.g., Jefferson Cup U9 Championship Format), so check your state's sanctioning rules.
- **Small-sided games run short** (often 20–25-minute halves). That lets 7v7/9v9 flights fit 4 games in a weekend, so 5-team full round robins and Gold/Silver "everyone advances" brackets are more common at these ages.

### 2-day vs 3-day (and multi-day) structures
- **2-day weekend (Sat–Sun):** 2 group games Saturday, 1 group game Sunday morning, then a final (or semifinal + final) Sunday afternoon. The practical cap is about 4–5 games for 7v7/9v9 and about 4 for 11v11. That's why 8-team flights often send only group winners to a final (Surf Cup), and why 12/16-team flights need semifinals the same day as the last group game.
  - Las Vegas Mayor's Cup: Girls U8–U14 play "3/2" (3 games over 2 days).\[67\]
  - Jefferson U9–U14 play Saturday–Sunday.\[58\]
- **3-day (Fri–Sun):** allows one group game per day plus a knockout, or 4-game showcases.
  - USYS Presidents Cup templates schedule group play over "Day 1 Day 2 Day 3."\[1\]
  - Surf Cup 2026 Olders ran June 12–15.\[13\]
- **Week-long:**
  - USYS Nationals 2026: three group days, rest day, quarterfinals, semifinals, rest day, finals (July 13–20 for 16U–19U boys).\[68\]\[69\]
  - The old East Regional: "6 or 7 day period," quarterfinals on day 4, semifinals day 6, finals day 7.\[8\]
  - Gothia and Dana run Monday/Tuesday to Saturday with A/B knockouts.\[24\]\[40\]
- **The field-count constraint:** a flight of *n* teams in groups of 4 needs about 1.5×n group matches. A 12-team flight needs 18 group slots plus 3 knockouts = 21 field-slots. With about 6–7 slots per field per day for 11v11, one 12-team flight occupies roughly 1.5 fields for a whole weekend.

  - Formats where "teams do not play the same number of games" can't use a standard template. Directors build a custom one (rounds, brackets, teams per bracket, number of meetings), or "import a GotSoccer Legacy template."\[10\]
  - Playoff templates are built from "tiers" and can be single-leg or "Two-Leg."\[70\]

- **TotalGlobalSports (TGS):** public pages mention a "drag and drop scheduler" and conflict warnings.\[72\] I couldn't find a public bracket-template catalog.

## Pros, Cons, and Fairness Quirks

| Format | Pros | Cons / fairness quirks |
|---|---|---|
| Single 4-team RR, no final | Simple; everyone plays everyone; 3 games | Title can be decided by tiebreakers; final-day games can be "dead rubbers" (meaningless) |
| RR + final | Climactic finish | The final is often a rematch of a group game; 3rd/4th get no 4th game |
| 5-team full RR | Most complete comparison | 4 games is a lot for a weekend; bye days create uneven rest |
| Partial RR (one team plays 4) | Keeps the 3-game guarantee | The 4th game "doesn't count," which is awkward; uneven fatigue |
| 2×3 crossover | Gets 3-team groups to 3 games | You never play your own group; semifinal rematches are possible (USYS admits "Semi-finalists could have played each other") |
| Mixed 4- and 3-team groups | Handles odd counts | 3-team groups play fewer games, so points must be averaged or the wildcard race is unequal. Strength of schedule differs |
| Winners + wildcard | Uses all 4 semifinal spots | A strong runner-up in a "group of death" can lose out to a weaker group's runner-up. The 10-point system can let a goal-heavy loser beat a tie-heavy team |
| "Best Nth place" across groups | Scales to big events | Only fair if group sizes match; goal-difference caps matter a lot |
| Gold/Silver all-advance | Everyone plays 4+ games; meaningful games for all | Bottom teams can "win silver" after losing every group game; more fields needed |
| Straight knockout | Fewest games; maximal drama | Losers get 1 game unless there's consolation; heavy luck from draws |
| Showcase (no playoffs) | Predictable schedule; good for scouting | Low stakes; standings may still exist, which confuses families |
| League phase (Gothia) | More varied opponents than groups | Not everyone plays the same strength schedule; complex tiebreakers |

**Specific quirks worth knowing:**
- **"Wildcard math" eliminations:** in 3×4 formats, two runners-up with identical points are separated by goal difference, which is capped. If both are capped, it goes to goals allowed and then penalties or a coin toss. Surf Cup's 3+-team tie procedure even advances teams progressively ("Team E is eliminated, and Teams B, C and D are evaluated under the next criteria").\[13\]\[73\]
- **The incentive problem:** with "most wins" ahead of goal difference (USYS/Arizona), a team that won 1 and lost 2 beats a team that drew all 3 games on equal points.
- **The sportsmanship problem in the 10-point system:** teams chase goals 1–3 and the shutout, so a 3-0 win (10 points) is worth much more than a 1-0 win (8 points).
- **Uneven rest:** in templates with byes (USYS 7/9/11/13), some teams get rest days and others don't.

## Recommendations (for the user reading tournament packets)

1. **Look up your flight's team count in the catalog above, then confirm it against the event's rules PDF for *this* season.** Formats and tiebreakers change yearly, and many rules documents still carry dates or wording from earlier years.
2. **For 12-team flights,** assume 3 groups of 4 → winners + 1 wildcard → semifinals unless the event says otherwise. Your team needs to win its group, or win it on goal difference with a shutout. Being second rarely gets you through.
3. **Check four things in every rules packet:**
   - points system (3-1-0 vs 10-point);
   - the goal-difference cap (3, 4, 5, or none);
   - whether head-to-head comes first;
   - whether knockout ties go straight to penalties.
   These four details decide most tight advancements.
4. **If you're designing a tournament,** start from the USYS Presidents Cup Protocol 515 templates. They're the closest thing to an official, defensible format per team count. Use crossover for 3-team groups instead of 2-game groups if your fields allow it.

## Caveats
- Rules cited span 2019–2026. The 2025-26 USYS rules (March 2026), the 2026 Arizona State Cup rules (updated Feb 27, 2026), the 2026 Surf Cup rules, ECNL/GA 2025-26 and 2026-27 announcements, and Gothia/Dana 2026 are current. The USYS East Region (2023-24) structure is now **historical**: per USYS's Sept. 23, 2025 announcement, State Cup champions now advance directly to the National Championships, "eliminating the traditional Regional Championship round" (USYS Regional Presidents Cup qualifiers continue). It's still a useful template for 18/20/24-team counts.
- I couldn't access Dallas Cup's current rules PDF, the public text of GotSport's standard template names, or any TGS/SincSports/Affinity/Demosphere/PlayMetrics/TourneyCentral template catalogs. Statements about those platforms are limited to what their public help pages say.
- Some examples come from small local events (Nebraska, Wisconsin, Texas, Washington, 3v3 operators). They show real-world variants but aren't national standards.
- MLS NEXT Cup's 48-team 2027 bracket and GA's 2026-27 final-stage details had been announced but not fully detailed at the time of writing. Treat them as planned, not played.
- Total-game counts in the table are my arithmetic from the published structures, assuming no forfeits, weather changes or added consolation games.
