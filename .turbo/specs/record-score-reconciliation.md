# Record↔Score Reconciliation (two-sided, published-score layer)

## Overview

The published ranking board over-weights strength-of-schedule. Mediocre-record teams ride brutal schedules too high (Strikers 47% win / 0.99 SOS at #4) while genuinely strong teams on softer schedules sit too low (SOLAR 90% win at #20, De Anza 67% at #70, Michigan #84). Against the owner's 23-team u16M/u17F expert ground truth, production scores **7/23**.

The prior attempt — a record-gated, one-sided SOS-credit cap that only *lowers* `powerscore_core` — improved this to a best of **14/23** but failed the gate at every strength for three structural reasons: (1) a single global cap strength can't serve both cohorts, (2) being one-sided it cannot *lift* genuinely-strong-but-buried teams, and (3) within-cohort z-scored record compressed strong records in elite cohorts, so the strength needed to drop a 47%-win team also caught 63%/90%-win genuine elites. Full evidence: `.turbo/sos-credit-cap-scorecard.md`.

This spec defines a **two-sided, record-anchored reconciliation** in the published-score layer: pull SOS-inflated teams *down* toward their record-expected level **and** lift record-justified buried teams *up* toward it. It is **asymmetric** (the upward lift is smaller and more conservative than the downward pull, because the larger credibility risk is "obviously wrong teams too high"), **per-cohort self-normalizing** (no single global number), and **gated on record** so genuine elites are never collateral and losing teams are never rewarded. `mu` is untouched — a mu-level lift is a documented escalation reserved for if the published-layer lift cannot rescue the deepest buried teams. The mechanism is flag-gated default-off (byte-identical to prod when off) and validated zero-prod-write. Delivery is **staged**: the down-side separation first, the lift second. The default-off foundation (hook, config, staging dials, tests) is already committed on the unmerged `scf-off-staging` worktree branch, not `main` (see Design → Branch baseline).

## Requirements

### Reconciliation core

- **R1.** When a team's published `powerscore_core` exceeds its **record-expected level on the core scale** (`record_expected_core`, the commensurate conversion defined in R2) by more than the cohort-calibrated tolerance, the system shall reduce it toward that level (the refined separation that replaces the global-MAX cap).
- **R2.** The system shall compute `record_expected` as an absolute-anchored index of win-rate (primary) and goal-differential (secondary) mapped to [0,1] by **absolute** thresholds — so a genuinely strong record (e.g. ≥~85% win) maps near the top in *any* cohort. The record basis is **windowed and draw-aware**: it uses **all** of the team's games within the rankings lookback window (`WINDOW_DAYS`), **not** the selected ≤30-game rating subset, and counts draws as first-class (e.g. points-based `(W + 0.5·D)/games`), not `wins/games_played`. The system shall then **explicitly convert** `record_expected` into a value commensurate with the cohort-relative `powerscore_core` scale — `record_expected_core` — **before any down-pull math or tuning**; equal [0,1] range is not sufficient (see Design → Scale crosswalk). The conversion shall preserve the absolute top-anchoring (a strong record stays high in any cohort).
- **R3.** The reconciliation strength shall derive from each cohort's own dispersion of (`powerscore_core` − `record_expected_core`) — the *converted* gap, self-normalizing, with no single global constant and no per-cohort hand-tuned table — so one mechanism serves every cohort.
- **R4.** All cohort statistics that drive reconciliation (`record_expected` and its `record_expected_core` conversion, the dispersion-derived tolerance, the lift quantile, and the (`record_expected_core` − current) gap) shall be computed once from a **frozen pre-reconciliation snapshot** of the cohort, and the down-pull and lift shall be applied in a **single pass** with no within-run recomputation — so the result is order-independent and idempotent.
- **R5.** A team with record-justified high standing (strong record **and** high score, e.g. Total Futbol 80%/0.99) shall not be reduced.

### Lift (second stage)

- **R6.** Where a team clears **all three** of (a) an absolute win-rate floor, (b) a top record quantile within its cohort, and (c) a positive (`record_expected_core` − current) gap, the system shall raise its published score toward `record_expected_core` — bounded, and by a strictly smaller maximum than the down-pull tolerance (asymmetric, conservative-up).
- **R7.** The lift shall be structurally incapable of raising a team that fails the win-rate floor; the losing-in-top-decile guardrail shall not regress.

### Safety & validation

- **R8.** All behavior shall sit behind a default-off flag; with the flag off the published board shall be byte-identical to production — verified by the unchanged engine unit suite **and** a flag-off full board that is **byte-identical, row-for-row, to a same-snapshot reconcile-off baseline**. The observed ground-truth score on that baseline (currently ~7/23) is recorded as **evidence**, not a fixed contract (the absolute number is snapshot-dependent and ages).
- **R9.** Candidates shall be built into named scratch tables via the existing staging harness from a single fetch-once snapshot, scored read-only (zero prod-write).
- **R10a.** **Down-side ship gate (Stage 1, deterministic).** Evaluated on the **full-pipeline staging board** — `rank_in_cohort_final`, i.e. after ML, raw shrink, publish penalties and publication caps, NOT the raw engine score — scored by `data/staging/audit_ground_truth.py` against the checked-in fixture `data/staging/audit_ground_truth.json` (the fixture encodes a `target_min` rank floor per "drop" team and `cur` per "elite" team). A candidate passes when: every "drop" team reaches its `target_min` floor; **no** "elite" team currently within the scorer's elite ceiling (`--elite-ceiling`, default top 15) falls out of it (no genuine-elite demotion); the `scripts/diagnose_bubble_teams.py` bubble count and `data/staging/score_losing_top_decile.py` losing-in-top-decile count do not regress versus a same-snapshot cap-off baseline; **and no pre-identified buried-elite fixture team falls materially below its same-snapshot reconcile-off baseline rank** (a buried-elite non-regression bound — the absolute anchor must leave genuine buried elites untouched; this guards the down-only failure mode where a buried elite is driven *further* down, e.g. Michigan 84→157 under the old cohort-relative cap). The elite-RISE outcome is **not** required here — a down-only stage cannot lift (that is R10b). This is Stage 1's complete, executable ship bar.
- **R10b.** **Lift gate (Stage 2).** On the same full-pipeline board, every buried "elite" team **rises materially** versus its current rank, to an owner-set per-elite rise target. The current scorer only tests `cand_rank ≤ --elite-ceiling`, which cannot express "rise materially"; Stage 2 shall **extend `data/staging/audit_ground_truth.py` and `audit_ground_truth.json`** to encode a per-elite rise target (a minimum rank improvement, or a reachable top-N). **These targets are pre-registered — fixed in the checked-in fixture before the Stage 2 sweep begins, exactly like the `target_min` drop floors; the sweep calibrates the mechanism's constants to clear that fixed bar, never the bar to fit the results.** R10b is not measurable until that scorer/fixture extension lands. **Because the combined two-sided mechanism is what ships, the Stage 2 release gate is R10a AND R10b** — the lift must not reintroduce any R10a regression (a drop escaping its floor, a genuine-elite demotion, or a guardrail regression).
- **R11.** **Prediction guardrail (engine-level).** Before any ship — Stage 1 or Stage 2 — the mechanism shall be ported into the prediction-backtest fork (`experiments/glicko_backtest/glicko_engine_exp.py`, whose `powerscore_adj` step differs from prod — it shrinks toward 0.5 via `PROVISIONAL_SHRINK_TO_MEAN`) with a variant, and the variant's **published-score ordering** metric (`ord_acc_ps_adj`, `ord_acc_ps_core`) shall be not materially worse versus `baseline_prod`. **Scope limit:** the backtest fork runs only the engine — it does NOT apply the downstream ML / publish penalties / publication caps — so this metric is an engine-level proxy; the authoritative published-ordering gate is **R10a/R10b on the full-pipeline board**. The `mu`-based pooled accuracy and log-loss are an **invariant sanity check** only (the mechanism never touches `mu`, so they must stay ~identical; drift signals an unintended leak into `mu`). The backtest shall run against the **same engine code as the candidate**: the fork is copied into the worktree (or the worktree engine synced into the fork) and the port kept in lockstep with `src/etl/glicko_engine.py` on the branch — never validate one tree while implementing another.

## Design

**Architecture.** A single published-score post-`mu` shaping stage on `powerscore_core`, extending the committed `apply_sos_credit_cap` hook (renamed/generalized to a record-score reconciliation) in `src/etl/glicko_engine.py`. It runs within each cohort after the `powerscore_core` if/else converges and before `powerscore_adj`. It never touches `mu`, `mu_sos`, SOS, SCF, cross-age anchors, evidence gates, or ML. (R1, R8)

**Branch baseline.** The default-off foundation this extends — the `apply_sos_credit_cap` hook, the `SOS_CREDIT_CAP_ENABLED` config, the `run_scf_off_staging.py` `--sos-credit-cap`/`--sos-credit-max` dials, the `_cfg_dict` fingerprint keys, and the tests — is committed on the **unmerged** git worktree branch `scf-off-staging` (`C:/pitchrank-scf-off`, commit `5c4f44a3d`), **not** in `main`. Implementation extends that branch; a fresh checkout of `main` will not contain these symbols.

**Record-expected score (shared anchor).** An absolute-anchored monotone index: win-rate mapped to [0,1] by fixed thresholds (primary), goal-differential per game as a bounded secondary term. The record basis is **windowed and draw-aware** — all of the team's games within the rankings lookback window (`WINDOW_DAYS`), not the selected ≤30-game rating subset, with draws counted (points-based). Absolute (not within-cohort z) anchoring is the fix for the elite-cohort compression that demoted SOLAR/ALBION. (R2, R4)

**Scale crosswalk (commensurability).** `powerscore_core` is a *cohort-relative* score (`sigmoid_zscore_normalize`, centered on the cohort mean, with SOS/SCF/evidence shaping baked in); `record_expected` is *absolute*. They share the [0,1] range but not the scale, so a raw `powerscore_core − record_expected` is meaningless — a cohort whose records sit systematically above/below 0.5 would be pulled wholesale. Before any down-pull, `record_expected` is therefore **explicitly converted** into a `powerscore_core`-comparable value `record_expected_core` via a per-cohort **affine projection**: `record_expected_core = cohort_core_center + β · (record_expected − R0)`, where `cohort_core_center` is the cohort's `powerscore_core` center (which absorbs the per-cohort offset) and `(record_expected − R0)` is the team's **absolute** record deviation from a fixed baseline `R0` (which preserves the absolute top-anchoring — a strong record yields a high `record_expected_core` in *any* cohort, with no elite-cohort compression). `β`, `R0`, and the tolerance are sweep-tuned within this fixed shape; cohort-relativity enters only through the center, never through the anchor. Both the down-pull and the lift reconcile toward `record_expected_core`, computed once from the frozen pre-reconciliation snapshot. (R2, R3, R4)

**Down-pull (stage 1).** `core` is reduced toward `record_expected_core` only when it exceeds `record_expected_core` by more than a cohort-self-normalized tolerance derived from that cohort's (`powerscore_core` − `record_expected_core`) dispersion. Strong records (high `record_expected_core`) keep full credit; the reduction is one-sided (never raises) and continuous (no rank cliff). (R1, R3, R4, R5)

**Lift (stage 2).** For teams passing the layered eligibility gate (absolute win-rate floor AND top record quantile AND positive gap), `core` is raised toward `record_expected_core`, bounded by a maximum strictly smaller than the down-pull tolerance. The absolute win-rate floor makes rewarding a losing team structurally impossible. (R4, R6, R7)

**Config & harness.** New default-off env-backed flag(s) mirroring the existing `SOS_CREDIT_CAP_ENABLED` pattern; extend the `_cfg_dict` cache fingerprint; extend `scripts/run_scf_off_staging.py` with the down/up dials. Validate through `data/staging/audit_ground_truth.py` (primary gate, on the full-pipeline board `rank_in_cohort_final` against its JSON fixture — Stage 2 extends the scorer + fixture for the R10b elite-rise rule), `scripts/diagnose_bubble_teams.py` and `data/staging/score_losing_top_decile.py` (guardrails), and — for the backtest gate — the fork port + variant run against the **same tree** as the candidate. (R8, R9, R10a, R10b, R11)

**Flag-off identity.** The reconciliation stage is invoked only under the flag; off → the function is never called and `powerscore_core` (and `power_presos`) are untouched. (R8)

## MVP Scope

Staged, each stage validated independently on the same fetch-once snapshot:

- **Stage 1 — refined separation (down-side):** R1–R5 (down-side core, incl. frozen-stats and no-demotion), plus R8, R9, **R10a**, R11. One-sided (lowers only). **Ships on its own as an independent release** once it clears the deterministic **R10a down-side gate** and the R11 prediction guardrail — it is the most important credibility win (removes the indefensible top-10 teams without demoting genuine elites). Success target: beat the prior cap's down-side **without** the genuine-elite demotion that sank it.
- **Stage 2 — lift (up-side):** add R6, R7, and **R10b** (which includes extending the ground-truth scorer + fixture, with the per-elite rise targets pre-registered before the sweep). The combined two-sided mechanism is what ships, so its release gate is **R10a AND R10b** — the lift must re-prove the Stage 1 down-side contract still holds (no drop escapes its floor, no elite demoted, guardrails not regressed). R11 (backtest) becomes **load-bearing**.

**Deferred / escalation (out of scope here):** a `mu`-level lift, considered only if the published-layer lift in Stage 2 cannot raise the deepest buried elites enough to clear the directional+magnitude bar.

## Open Questions

These need the staging sweep against the snapshot; resolve during stage implementation rather than guessing now. (Provisional defaults set at implementation; the mechanism constants are tuned to the lowest-intervention values that clear **R10a** for Stage 1 and **R10a + R10b** for Stage 2 — distinct from the R10b per-elite rise targets, which are the fixed success bar pre-registered in the fixture before the sweep, not tuned.)
