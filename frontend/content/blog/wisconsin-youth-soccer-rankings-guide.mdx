# Wisconsin Youth Soccer Rankings: The Complete Parent's Guide (2026)

Two Wisconsin U14 teams can play a full season in the same state and hardly share an opponent. One trains in Hudson, across the St. Croix River from the Twin Cities, and plays almost all of its games against Minnesota teams. Another plays in the Milwaukee area and splits its schedule between Wisconsin and Illinois.

That's Wisconsin youth soccer: a state whose club game leans outward in two directions, west toward Minnesota and south toward Chicago. Your league table tells you how you did in your own corner of that map. Rankings are how you find out what it means across the whole state.

## Why Wisconsin Soccer Rankings Matter

In Wisconsin, "who's the best team in the state" is hard to settle from results alone, because the state's strongest programs play so many of their games against out-of-state teams. A Milwaukee-area team can build its record against Illinois and Michigan opponents while a western Wisconsin team builds one against Minnesota sides, and the two may never meet before the State Championships.

Rankings give you three things standings can't:

1. **Cross-state context** — How does your Madison 56ers or Bavarian United team actually compare to an FC Wisconsin or Elmbrook United side that plays a different schedule?
2. **Informed club decisions** — At tryouts, rankings let you compare FC Wisconsin, Elmbrook United, Bavarian United, Madison 56ers and SC Wave on one scale instead of on reputation.
3. **Realistic expectations** — Winning your local flight is a starting point. ECNL, MLS NEXT, Girls Academy, the State Championships and the US Youth Soccer National Championships are where the number gets tested.

## How Wisconsin Soccer Rankings Actually Work

Points-based systems such as GotSoccer award points for where a team places in tournaments and league flights, which can reward volume over quality. A team that enters 15 events and wins weak brackets can outrank a team that plays a harder schedule at fewer events.

### What PitchRank Tracks for Wisconsin Teams

We track **every game we can find** — league play, tournaments, friendlies, showcases — across all 1,789 Wisconsin teams we follow.

Here's the short version of how your team's ranking is calculated:

1. **Base rating** — Every team starts neutral and moves with each game played
2. **Strength of schedule** — Beating a top ECNL side means more than beating a developmental team. Losing to a strong opponent hurts less than losing to a weak one.
3. **Recency** — Last month's games count more than games from 10 months ago
4. **Consistency** — Steady performance ranks higher than wild swings between blowout wins and bad losses

The result is a **PowerScore from 0 to 100** that lets you compare teams within the same age group at a glance.

Rankings aren't comparable across age groups. A U10 team ranked #2 in Wisconsin isn't in the same position as a U14 team ranked #2. Why?

- **Competition density** — U14 has 72 ranked teams; U10 has 3

- **Playing up** — Some teams compete a year up for tougher games, which changes what their record means

**Pro tip:** Always check your specific age group when looking at [Wisconsin soccer rankings](/rankings/wi).

### What Rankings Don't Tell You

Rankings measure competitive results. They can't measure:

- **Individual player development** — A top-ranked team might not be the right fit for your child's growth
- **Coaching quality** — Some lower-ranked teams have better developmental coaches than win-now programs
- **Team culture** — Your kid's enjoyment matters more than a number
- **College fit** — D3 coaches care about GPA and character more than PowerScore

If a club director sells you on rankings alone but can't explain their development philosophy, that's a red flag.

## How to Use Wisconsin Rankings When Choosing a Club

Rankings are one tool at tryout time — here's how to use them well.

### Questions to Ask Club Directors

When you're comparing FC Wisconsin vs Elmbrook United vs SC Wave, or Madison 56ers vs Forward Madison FC:

1. **"How do your teams' rankings trend over time?"** — Upward trends suggest good coaching. Flat or declining suggests stagnation.
2. **"What's the strength of schedule for this age group?"** — Are they playing real competition or filling the calendar with easy brackets?
3. **"How many of this team's games are against out-of-state teams?"** — In Wisconsin the answer ranges from under a tenth to more than nine in ten, and it shapes both your weekends and how well-tested the team's ranking is.
4. **"For older boys: what happens during the high school season?"** — Ask how the club schedules around fall high school soccer, and what the team plays in spring and summer.

### Finding the Right Competition Level

The best team for your child isn't always the highest-ranked one. Look for a team that:

- Plays opponents 10–20 ranking positions above AND below them
- Gives your child 30+ minutes per game
- Challenges without crushing confidence
- Fits your family's travel budget and schedule

### Red Flags

- **Cherry-picking opponents** — Teams that pad records against the same weak flight. Their ranking will plateau.
- **Wild ranking swings** — Could signal roster instability or inconsistent coaching
- **Ranking guarantees** — No legitimate club can promise a specific ranking

## West to Minnesota, South to Illinois

Here's what makes Wisconsin different. We've recorded **5,225 games** for ranked Wisconsin teams over the past year. Counting each team's own games, **about half (49%) were against other Wisconsin teams**; Illinois teams account for 19%, Minnesota 12% and Michigan 6%, and no other state reaches 3%.

That statewide figure hides very different club patterns:

- **Western Wisconsin plays Minnesota.** Western Wisconsin Soccer plays 91% of its recorded games against Minnesota teams and only 6% against Wisconsin teams. Its teams play in Twin Cities Soccer Leagues, and its younger boys in ECNL RL Twin Cities.
- **The national-league clubs play across the region.** FC Wisconsin plays 28% of its games against Wisconsin teams and 29% against Illinois teams, with Michigan next. SC Wave spreads its schedule across Illinois, Minnesota and Michigan and plays about a quarter of its games against Wisconsin teams. Madison 56ers (38% Wisconsin, 27% Illinois, 19% Michigan) and Elmbrook United (42%, 25% and 15%) also play more than half their games against out-of-state teams.
- **Some clubs play mostly Wisconsin teams.** The Milwaukee Kickers play 92% of their recorded games against Wisconsin teams, and Sheboygan's Lakeshore United FC 74%.

What that means for parents:

- **A team with out-of-state opponents is rated against more of the region.** That's a strength, not a weakness — more distinct opponents means a better-supported rating.
- **A team that plays only Wisconsin teams is rated mostly against Wisconsin.** The rating still works, but it rests on a smaller, more connected pool. The State Championships and events with out-of-state teams are where it gets confirmed.
- **Two Wisconsin teams that never meet can still be compared**, because they share opponents in regional leagues and tournaments.

## Wisconsin State Championships and Rankings

Wisconsin Youth Soccer runs the State Championships for U13 through U17 and U19 in two seasons: boys U13–U19 and girls U13–U14 play in the spring, and girls U15–U19 in the fall. A 12U Junior State Championship and an 11U Junior Invitational don't advance to nationals. U13–U17 teams must play in a WYSA-approved league — the WYSA State League, MLS NEXT, ECNL, ECNL RL, GA, GA Aspire or National 1 — while U19 teams don't need one. Last year's champion and runner-up are seeded first and second, and everyone else is seeded on the previous year's State Championship and league results.

For spring 2027, group play is May 29–30 at the USA Youth Sports Complex in Appleton, with one group game played before that weekend, semifinals on May 31, and finals June 2–4 at Mindiola Park in Waukesha. Winners in U13 through U19 advance to the US Youth Soccer National Championships.

Where rankings fit in:

- **Seeding** — Prior State Championship and league results set the seeds, not your PitchRank ranking. Rankings show how those league results compare with teams outside your division
- **Competition quality** — The State Championships put western, Milwaukee-area and Madison programs in the same bracket, which doesn't happen often during the season
- **Exposure** — It's one of the few weekends where the state's regions are measured against each other under real stakes

If your team is ranked in the **top 10–15% in Wisconsin**, the State Championships are where that number gets tested against the rest of the state.

## The College Recruiting Reality Check

Let's be honest about what Wisconsin rankings mean for recruiting.

### What Coaches Actually Look At

1. **Individual highlight video** — YOUR child, not team stats
2. **Academic eligibility** — NCAA core-course GPA requirements and each college's own admission standards filter players before rankings matter
3. **Showcase attendance** — ECNL, MLS NEXT and Girls Academy events, where coaches can see many teams in one weekend
4. **Direct contact** — Emails to coaches with video links beat high rankings

### Rankings by Division

- **Division I and II** — A strong national rank helps a team get noticed, but coaches recruit individual players
- **Division III** — Rankings barely factor in. Academics, character, and fit drive decisions.

**Wisconsin's reality:** the state's ECNL, MLS NEXT and Girls Academy programs already test themselves against Midwest competition. Once your child hits U15, the national rank is the more useful number for recruiting conversations.
