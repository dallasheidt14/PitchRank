# Kansas Youth Soccer Rankings: The Complete Parent's Guide (2026)

Two Kansas U14 teams can spend a whole season in the same state and live in different soccer worlds. One plays out of Wichita and sees mostly other Wichita-area sides, week after week. The other trains in Overland Park, where the Missouri line is a few minutes away, and plays nearly as many Missouri teams as Kansas ones.

That's Kansas youth soccer: two metros roughly 200 miles apart on I-35, with very different schedules. Wichita's game is largely self-contained. The Kansas City side is one half of a metro split by a state line, and its clubs play across it constantly. Your league table tells you how you did in your corner of that map. Rankings are how you find out what it means across the whole state.

## Why Kansas Soccer Rankings Matter

In Kansas, "who's the best team in the state" is hard to settle from results alone, because the state's two big soccer centres rarely share a field. A Wichita team can post a strong record against local opposition while a Johnson County team posts a similar one against Missouri and Heartland opponents — and the two may never meet before State Cup.

Rankings give you three things standings can't:

1. **Cross-metro context** — How does your FC Wichita or Sporting Wichita team actually compare to a Kansas Rush or Sporting Blue Valley side out of Johnson County?
2. **Informed club decisions** — At tryouts, rankings let you compare FC Wichita, Sporting Wichita, Kansas Rush, Sporting Blue Valley and Union KC on one scale instead of on reputation.
3. **Realistic expectations** — Winning your local flight is a starting point. ECNL, MLS NEXT, State Cup and the US Youth Soccer National Championships are where the number gets tested.

## How Kansas Soccer Rankings Actually Work

Most ranking systems — GotSoccer being the biggest — award points for where a team places in tournaments and league flights, which can reward volume over quality. A team that enters 15 events and wins weak brackets can outrank a team that plays a harder schedule at fewer events.

### What PitchRank Tracks for Kansas Teams

We track **every game we can find** — league play, tournaments, friendlies, showcases — across all 1,170 Kansas teams we follow.

Here's the short version of how your team's ranking is calculated:

1. **Base rating** — Every team starts neutral and moves with each game played
2. **Strength of schedule** — Beating a top ECNL side means more than beating a developmental team. Losing to a strong opponent hurts less than losing to a weak one.
3. **Recency** — Last month's games count more than games from 10 months ago
4. **Consistency** — Steady performance ranks higher than wild swings between blowout wins and bad losses

The result is a **PowerScore between 0.0 and 1.0** that lets you compare teams within the same age group at a glance.

## What Your Kansas Team's Ranking Actually Tells You

You check PitchRank and see your U14 team is ranked #12 in Kansas. What does that mean?

### State vs National Rankings

- **State rank** — Where your team stands among Kansas teams in the same age group and gender
- **National rank** — Where your team stands among all teams in that age group and gender nationwide

**Reality check:** Kansas's state boards mix two metros that seldom play each other. A Wichita team's state rank leans on a mostly in-state schedule, while a Johnson County team's leans on Missouri and Heartland opponents. Both are measured on the same scale, but if your team rarely leaves its own metro, the national rank and the teams around it are a useful second look.

### Age Group Matters More Than You Think

Rankings aren't comparable across age groups. A U10 team ranked #5 in Kansas isn't in the same position as a U14 team ranked #5. Why?

- **Competition density** — U14 has 74 ranked teams; U10 has 21
- **Development stages** — Rankings are more volatile in younger age groups
- **Playing up** — Some teams compete a year up for tougher games, which changes what their record means

**Pro tip:** Always check your specific age group when looking at [Kansas soccer rankings](/rankings/ks).

### What Rankings Don't Tell You

Rankings measure competitive results. They can't measure:

- **Individual player development** — A top-ranked team might not be the right fit for your child's growth
- **Coaching quality** — Some lower-ranked teams have better developmental coaches than win-now programs
- **Team culture** — Your kid's enjoyment matters more than a number
- **College fit** — D3 coaches care about GPA and character more than PowerScore

If a club director sells you on rankings alone but can't explain their development philosophy, that's a red flag.

## How to Use Kansas Rankings When Choosing a Club

Rankings are one tool at tryout time — here's how to use them well.

### Questions to Ask Club Directors

When you're comparing FC Wichita vs Sporting Wichita, or Kansas Rush vs Sporting Blue Valley vs Union KC:

1. **"How do your teams' rankings trend over time?"** — Upward trends suggest good coaching. Flat or declining suggests stagnation.
2. **"What's the strength of schedule for this age group?"** — Are they playing real competition or filling the calendar with easy brackets?
3. **"Where does this age group play, and how often does it leave the metro?"** — In Kansas the answer differs sharply between Wichita and Kansas City, and it shapes both your weekends and how well-tested the team's ranking is.
4. **"How many players from this group have moved into ECNL or MLS NEXT rosters?"** — Player progression matters more than team rank.

### Finding the Right Competition Level

The best team for your child isn't always the highest-ranked one. Look for a team that:

- Plays opponents 10–20 ranking positions above AND below them
- Gives your child 30+ minutes per game
- Challenges without crushing confidence
- Fits your family's travel budget and schedule

### Red Flags

- **Cherry-picking opponents** — Teams that pad records against the same weak flight. Their ranking will plateau.
- **Wild ranking swings** — Could signal roster instability or inconsistent coaching
- **Ranking guarantees** — No legitimate club can promise a specific ranking

## Wichita at Home, Kansas City Across the Line

Here's what makes Kansas different. Across the **5,641 games** we've recorded for ranked Kansas teams over the past year, **about two-thirds of their games (68%) were against other Kansas teams**. Missouri opponents account for 11%, and no other state reaches 3% — Oklahoma, Illinois, Texas, Nebraska and Iowa each sit around 2%.

That statewide figure hides two very different patterns:

- **Wichita** plays mostly at home. The Wichita-area teams filed under the Wichita Regional Soccer Association play 99% of their recorded games against Kansas opponents, and Wichita United FC 93%. Even the city's two national-league clubs, FC Wichita and Sporting Wichita, play about 60% of their games in state, with Missouri and Oklahoma the next most common opponents.
- **Johnson County and the Kansas City metro** play across the state line. Sporting Blue Valley plays only 13% of its recorded games against Kansas teams and 35% against Missouri teams. Kansas Rush (43% Kansas, 25% Missouri), Union KC (33% and 26%) and Kansas City Surf (30% and 23%) all play a large share of their schedules on the Missouri side or further afield.

What that means for parents:

- **A Kansas City-side team's ranking is tied closely to Missouri and Heartland opponents.** That's a strength, not a weakness — more distinct opponents means a better-supported rating — and it's why our Missouri guide describes so much of Kansas City's competitive game sitting on the Kansas side of the line.
- **A Wichita team that plays only in Wichita is rated mostly against Wichita.** The rating still works, but it rests on a smaller, more connected pool. State Cup and out-of-town events are where it gets confirmed.
- **Two Kansas teams that never meet can still be compared**, because Wichita clubs and Kansas City clubs share opponents in national leagues and regional events.

## Kansas State Cup and Rankings

Kansas Youth Soccer runs the Kansas State Cup in two parts. For 2026-27, the fall event is for girls 15U–17U and 19U, with group play November 5–8 and finals November 13–15, 2026 at Compass Minerals Sporting Fields in Kansas City, Kansas. The spring event plays group rounds there on May 13–14 and May 20–21, 2027, with the final weekend June 3–6 at the Garmin Olathe Soccer Complex. To enter, all teams and players must be registered with Kansas Youth Soccer, and teams must have played in a sanctioned league. State Cup champions in the 13U through 18U/19U age brackets go on to the US Youth Soccer National Championships.

Where rankings fit in:

- **Entry** — Registration and sanctioned league play, not your ranking, decide whether your team can enter State Cup. Rankings show how your league results compare with teams outside your division
- **Competition quality** — State Cup puts Wichita and Kansas City programs in the same bracket, which doesn't happen often during the season
- **Exposure** — It's one of the few weekends where the state's two metros are measured against each other under real stakes

If your team is ranked in the **top 10–15% in Kansas**, State Cup is where that number gets tested against the other half of the state.

## The College Recruiting Reality Check

Let's be honest about what Kansas rankings mean for recruiting.

### What Coaches Actually Look At

1. **Individual highlight video** — YOUR child, not team stats
2. **Academic eligibility** — NCAA core-course GPA requirements and each college's own admission standards filter players before rankings matter
3. **Showcase attendance** — ECNL, MLS NEXT and Girls Academy events, where coaches can see many teams in one weekend
4. **Direct contact** — Emails to coaches with video links beat high rankings

### Rankings by Division

- **Division I** — Coaches notice teams in the **top 5% nationally**
- **Division II** — Top 15–20% nationally gets attention, but individual performance matters more
- **Division III** — Rankings barely factor in. Academics, character, and fit drive decisions.

**Kansas's reality:** the state's ECNL, ECNL Regional League and MLS NEXT programs already test themselves against Heartland and national competition every season. Once your child hits U15, the national rank is the more useful number for recruiting conversations — especially for a Wichita team whose state rank comes mostly from in-state games.
